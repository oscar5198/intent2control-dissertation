Listening task

  • Read a short listening scenario and imagine yourself in that situation.
  • Listen to three different versions (mixes) of the same music audio clip.
  • You may listen in any order and replay versions as many times as needed.
  • Rate each version on a continuous 0–100 preference scale according to how well it matches the listening scenario.
  • Provide one required comment for each version.
  • For each listening scenario, you will listen to two different music audio clips. Each audio clip has three different versions (mixes), and you will rate and comment on all three versions before moving to the next audio clip.
  • The versions will use neutral labels such as Version A, Version B, and Version C.
  • You will not be told the underlying mix identities.
  • You can continue from each trial only after all required ratings and comments are complete.

Preference scale

Use the 0-100 scale to show your preference for each version. The endpoint wording remains governed by the approved study configuration; no midpoint label has been approved.
