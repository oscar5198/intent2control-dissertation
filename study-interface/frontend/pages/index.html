Music Mix Preferences in Everyday Listening Situations

An online listening study being conducted as part of an MSc dissertation at Queen Mary University of London.

This study explores listeners’ preferences for different versions of the same music in a range of everyday listening situations.

Participation is entirely voluntary. If you decide not to participate, this will not affect you in any way.

  • Expected duration: 20-25 minutes.
  • You must be aged 18 years or older to take part.
  • Please use headphones or earphones.
  • Please complete the study in a quiet listening environment.
  • Please use a comfortable listening volume.
