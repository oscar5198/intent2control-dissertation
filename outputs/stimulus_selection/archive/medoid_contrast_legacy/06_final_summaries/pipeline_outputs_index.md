# Pipeline Outputs Index

- `01_dataset_and_song_selection/`: dataset inventory, song ranking, and song-selection reports.
- `02_excerpt_selection/`: alignment tables, approved excerpt decision, preview audio, and diagnostics.
- `03_feature_extraction/`: Diff-MST feature tables, schema, reports, and diagnostics.
- `04_mix_selection/`: processed features, PCA, distances, triplet scores, selected previews, and figures.
- `05_manual_review/`: final human approval records and instructions.
- `06_final_summaries/`: compact human-facing summaries.
- `logs_and_provenance/`: migration, cleanup, naming, and reorganisation records.
- `archive_unused/`: superseded or legacy-layout files preserved for traceability.
