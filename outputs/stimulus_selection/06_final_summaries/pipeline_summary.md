# Pipeline Summary

## Stage 1: Dataset and Song Selection
The dataset inventory and song-selection outputs identify the final four songs: In The Meantime, Lead Me, Pouring Room, and Red To Blue.

## Stage 2: Excerpt Selection
Alignment and excerpt selection approved one 28-second aligned excerpt for each song.

## Stage 3: Feature Extraction
Validated Diff-MST descriptors were extracted for 92 retained human mixes. Retained mix counts: Lead Me 37; In The Meantime 36; Red To Blue 10; Pouring Room 9.

## Stage 4: Mix Selection
Robust scaling, Bark PCA, distance analysis, triplet scoring, and manual approval selected one representative and two contrasts per song.

Final selected original mix names:
- In The Meantime: representative UCP-A4; contrast_1 DU-J; contrast_2 QUT-B
- Lead Me: representative McG-A; contrast_1 PXL-L1; contrast_2 DU-D
- Pouring Room: representative McG-Q; contrast_1 McG-pro1; contrast_2 McG-X
- Red To Blue: representative McG-A; contrast_1 McG-B; contrast_2 McG-F

## Stage 5: Final Participant Stimuli
The final loudness-normalised participant stimuli are in `outputs/final_stimuli/stimuli/`.
