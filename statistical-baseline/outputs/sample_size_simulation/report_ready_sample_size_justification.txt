Phase 3C final-design simulation used rating ~ episode + group + z_RMS + z_CF + z_SW + (1 | participant_id) + (1 | stimulus_id). Candidate final N: 50. The requested 20 replications were reduced to 10: Fallback from 20 to 10 replications per condition because 360 repeated Bayesian fits was not computationally practical in this desktop execution.
